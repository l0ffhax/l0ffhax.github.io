Znixx privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Znixx is a puzzle game for Android: twist a snake of 24 wedges into the shape of the day. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your phone, we don't run any servers, and there are no analytics. Ads come from Google AdMob: short videos you choose to watch for extra help, and now and then an ad between levels.

What stays on your device

Your progress, personal bests, daily results and streak, achievements earned, saved shapes, snake colours and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads

Ads are provided by Google AdMob: short videos you choose to watch (for an extra hint, the step-by-step guide, or to try every snake finish), and now and then an ad between Levels or Story puzzles. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about the ad and your device, under the Google advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent before any ad, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Znixx+ removes all ads.

Purchases (optional)

Znixx+ is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see them. The game only asks Google Play whether Znixx+ is owned on your Google account, and remembers the answer on your device.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games profile (your player ID, display name and avatar) together with the scores and achievements the game submits for you. Your display name and scores appear on the game's public leaderboards. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we don't get access to it beyond what is needed to show the leaderboards in the game. You can play without signing in, and you can manage or delete your Play Games data in your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Sharing shapes

When you share a shape, the game creates a short code and link that contain only the shape and the name you give it. It is sent with the app you pick (for example a messaging app), under that app's privacy terms. We don't see what you share.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Crash and performance data

Google Play may collect crash and performance reports from your device, depending on your device settings, and share an aggregated, anonymised summary with us to help fix problems. The game itself doesn't include any crash-reporting or analytics software.

Children

Znixx is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
